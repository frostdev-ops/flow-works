flow-works

Open source · for Degree Works students

Your degree,
as a flowchart.

Flow-works turns your Degree Works audit into an animated, semester-by-semester map of what you've finished, what you're taking now, and a plan for everything left, with prerequisites drawn in.

  1. Flow-worksDrag this button to your bookmarks bar.
  2. Open your Degree Works worksheet and sign in as usual.
  3. Click the bookmark. Your flowchart opens here.
See the demo →

Everything runs in your browser. Your audit is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Does it work with my school?

Flow-works reads the Ellucian Degree Works Responsive Dashboard. If your worksheet address contains /responsivedash/ or /responsiveDashboard/, it should work. If it doesn't, open an issue.

How is the plan made?

Remaining requirements go into upcoming terms, lowest course level first, never before their prerequisites, up to your credit limit per term. Treat it as a starting point for a conversation with your advisor. It isn't a registration plan.

Is my data safe?

The bookmarklet reads the audit you're already viewing and hands it to this page in the URL fragment (the part after #), which browsers never send to servers. The page keeps it in your browser's local storage until you load another audit. The code is open source, so you can check this yourself.
